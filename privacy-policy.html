Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GymLog ("GymLog", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and discloses personal data when you use the GymLog web application (the "Service"). It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1. Who we are

GymLog is operated by an independent developer based in Singapore. We are responsible for the personal data processed through the Service. You can contact us at pbarath29@gmail.com.

2. Personal data we collect

2.1 Account information

When you create an account, the following is processed by Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google:

  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, profile photo URL and a unique user identifier.
  • If you sign up with email: your email address, your password and a unique user identifier. Your password is stored by Firebase Authentication in hashed form. We never see or have access to it.
  • The date your account was created and the date you last signed in, which Firebase Authentication records automatically.

Your name and profile photo are used only to display your account inside the Service. They are held by Firebase Authentication and are not copied into our database.

2.2 Content you create

  • Workout records: the days you plan or log, whether a day is a rest day, muscle groups, exercise names, and for each set the weight, number of repetitions and whether it was completed.
  • Collections: workout routines you save, including any starter routines you add.
  • Progress photos: images you choose to upload, with the date they were added. Before upload, each photo is resized on your device to a maximum of 600 pixels on its longest side and compressed as a JPEG image.
  • Settings: your chosen weight unit (kg or lb), whether you have completed the in-app tutorial, and which starter routines you have added or removed.

2.3 Collections you share

If you choose to share a collection, a separate copy is created containing the collection's name, its muscle groups, exercises, sets, weights and repetitions, your weight unit, the time it was created, and your unique user identifier (so that only you can delete it). It does not contain your name, email address, profile photo, workout history or progress photos. See section 5 for who can view it.

2.4 Usage information

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Service is used. It collects:

  • Records of the following actions: creating an account and signing in (including whether Google or email was used); completing a workout (including the number of exercises, sets and personal records, but not exercise names, weights or repetitions); achieving a personal record; applying a routine to a day; sharing or importing a collection; and the outcome of the prompt to install the app.
  • Information collected automatically by Google Analytics, such as pages viewed, session length, device type, operating system, browser, screen size, language and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • A randomly generated identifier stored in a cookie in your browser, used to recognise repeat visits.

We do not send your name, email address or account identifier to Google Analytics, and we do not use it for advertising.

2.5 Technical information

Our hosting provider and Google's services automatically receive technical information needed to deliver the Service, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of each request, which may be recorded in their server logs.

3. How we use your personal data

We use personal data only to:

  • create and maintain your account and let you sign in;
  • store your workouts, collections, photos and settings and make them available on each device you sign in on;
  • provide features that rely on your history, such as previous-session figures, personal records, streaks, strength charts and workout summaries;
  • let you share collections with others and add collections shared with you;
  • send emails you request, such as password reset emails, through Firebase Authentication;
  • understand how the Service is used so that we can maintain and improve it;
  • keep the Service secure, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable law.

We do not sell your personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Where your data is stored and how it is protected

Your account information is stored by Firebase Authentication, and your workouts, collections, progress photos and settings are stored in Google Cloud Firestore. These services are operated by Google and data may be stored and processed on servers outside Singapore. Where personal data is transferred outside Singapore, we rely on our service providers' contractual commitments to protect it to a standard comparable to that required under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012.

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to stored data is restricted by security rules so that each account can read and write only its own data, apart from shared collections as described in section 5. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4.1 Information stored on your device

So that the Service can open and record workouts without an internet connection, your browser stores:

  • a copy of your account data (workouts, collections, photos you have viewed and settings), which is sent to our servers when you are next online;
  • your sign-in session, so you remain signed in;
  • the app's own files, so it can load without a connection;
  • small preferences such as your weight unit, your chosen chart range, whether you have completed the tutorial and whether the install prompt has been shown.

Signing out or deleting your account does not remove the copy stored in your browser. If you use a shared device, you can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

5. When your data is disclosed

  • Service providers: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Google Analytics for Firebase), and Render, which hosts the Service. They process data on our behalf to provide their services. Their handling of data is also governed by their own terms and privacy policies, including Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Shared collections: a collection you share can be viewed, and copied into their own account, by any signed-in GymLog user who has its link. The copy remains available until you delete your account.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose personal data where required by law, regulation, legal process or a lawful request by a public authority, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or us.

We do not otherwise disclose your personal data to third parties.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account information and content for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your workouts, collections, progress photos, settings, shared collections and Firebase Authentication account are deleted. Google may retain residual copies for a limited period as part of its own deletion and backup processes.

Usage information in Google Analytics is not linked to your account and is kept for the retention period configured for the Service in Google Analytics, after which it is deleted automatically.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your workouts, collections and settings, and view or delete your photos, at any time in the Service.
  • Export: you can download a copy of your workouts, collections, progress photos and weight unit setting from Settings > Export My Data.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account and its data from Settings > Delete Account. This requires an internet connection, and for security you may be asked to sign in again first.
  • Withdrawing consent: you may withdraw your consent to our use of your personal data by deleting your account. We will then no longer be able to provide the Service to you.
  • Other requests: to request access to or correction of personal data that you cannot manage in the Service, or to ask about how we handle your data, contact us at pbarath29@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity first.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. If we make material changes, we will take reasonable steps to bring them to your attention. Your continued use of the Service after an update takes effect means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

For questions, requests or complaints about this Privacy Policy or our handling of personal data, contact us at pbarath29@gmail.com.
