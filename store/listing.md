## Main store listing

**App name** (max 30 characters)

**Short description** (max 80 characters)
Log your lifts, beat last time, and share workouts. Free and works offline.

**Full description** (max 4000 characters)

LastMark is a simple, fast workout log for people who lift. Plan your week, log every set, and always know what to beat.

KNOW WHAT TO BEAT
• Last session's weight and reps show under every set. Tap them to fill the set in.
• Beat your best and the exercise gets a PR badge.
• Works for bodyweight exercises too, like pull-ups and dips.

PLAN YOUR WEEK
• Each day of the week has its own workout. Mark rest days and see your whole month at a glance.
• Start from a ready-made routine (Push, Pull, Legs, Upper, Lower, Full Body) or build your own.
• Save your routines and apply them to any day in one tap. Copy last week's session just as quickly.
• Reorder exercises, and get exercise name suggestions as you type.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Strength chart for every exercise over 3 months, 6 months, a year or all time.
• Workouts this week, your weekly streak and your total workouts.
• Body weight log with its own trend chart.
• Progress photos grouped by month. Compare any two side by side.

SHARE YOUR WORKOUTS
• Turn any workout into a share image for your story or feed: total volume, sets, PRs and your lifts.
• Dark, orange, or your own photo behind the stats. Story or square size.
• Share your routines with friends by link.

WORKS IN THE GYM
• Logs with no signal and syncs when you're back online.
• Syncs across your phone, tablet and computer.
• Weights in kg or lb.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• No ads.
• Export all your workouts, routines, photos and body weight as a file at any time.
• Delete your account and all your data from Settings.

LastMark is a record-keeping tool and does not provide medical or fitness advice.

## Data safety

**Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** Yes
**Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?** Yes (HTTPS/TLS)
**Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?** Yes, in the app (Settings > Delete Account) and by email.

Data **shared** with third parties: **None.** Google (Firebase) and Render process data on LastMark's behalf as service providers, which Play does not count as sharing.

Data **collected**:

| Play category | Data type | Required or optional | Purposes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info | Name | Optional (only with Google sign-in) | Account management |
| Personal info | Email address | Required | Account management |
| Personal info | User IDs | Required | Account management, App functionality |
| Health and fitness | Fitness info (workouts, sets, weights, reps, body weight) | Required for the app to work | App functionality |
| Photos and videos | Photos (progress photos the user uploads) | Optional | App functionality |
| App activity | App interactions (e.g. workout completed, routine applied, share link created) | Required | Analytics |
| Device or other IDs | Device or other IDs (Google Analytics browser identifier) | Required | Analytics |
| Location | Approximate location (derived from IP address by Google Analytics and Firebase Authentication) | Required | Analytics, Fraud prevention and security |

Not collected: precise location, contacts, messages, audio, files, calendar, financial info, health info (medical), web browsing history, crash logs.

Notes:
- Photos chosen in **Share workout** stay on the device and are not uploaded. Only progress photos added on the Progress/Photos pages are stored.
- "Is this data processed ephemerally?" **No.** It's stored until the user deletes it or their account.
